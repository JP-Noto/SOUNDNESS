# SOUNDNESS

**The record states nothing the source piece does not vouch for.**

`Status: Public Draft` · `Corpus: 0.1` · `Rank: hypothesis` · `License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0` · French documents are
authoritative: [README.fr.md](README.fr.md).

> From the logic term: a proof system is sound when everything provable in it is true. Applied
> here to knowledge extracted from documents.

## What it studies

When a machine extracts working knowledge from documents, trust dies one of two ways: by
dilution (no structure, every pass reads differently, verified and generated blur) or by
rigidity (a schema decreed upfront, real documents overflow it, the system gets bypassed).
SOUNDNESS claims trust lives neither in the copy nor in the schema, but in the path that links
the copy to what vouches for it: three commitments, none sufficient without the other two.

1. **Duality**: the extracted record is never the original; it points to its source piece, the
   only opposable one, and any judgment grounded in a consultation is traced.
2. **The living template**: structure is born minimal (the smallest useful schema) and grows
   through accumulated validation acts; volume proposes, the act decides.
3. **Traveling confidence**: every record carries its extraction status (born-conforming,
   extracted, curated, validated), and whatever the assistant reads carries the confidence of
   what it reads.
